# University Academic Regulations & Student Handbook

## Section 1: Attendance Requirements

Students are expected to attend all scheduled lectures, tutorials, and laboratory sessions.
**Clause 1.1:** To be eligible to sit for any final examination, a student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in that specific module. There are absolutely no exceptions to this baseline requirement.
**Clause 1.2:** Tardiness exceeding 15 minutes will be recorded as a full absence for that session.

## Section 2: Medical Exemptions and Absences

The University recognizes that students may face unforeseen health emergencies.
**Clause 2.1:** If a student suffers a documented medical emergency, they must submit a valid medical certificate within 48 hours of the missed session.
**Clause 2.2:** A valid medical exemption officially waives all attendance requirements for exam eligibility for the duration of the illness.

## Section 3: Fee Deadlines and Penalties

| Fee Type       | Due Date      | Late Penalty        |
| :------------- | :------------ | :------------------ |
| Tuition (Fall) | September 1st | $50 after Oct 1st   |
| Hostel/Dorm    | August 15th   | $100 after Sept 1st |
| Library Fines  | End of Term   | Degrees Withheld    |

**Clause 3.4:** All late penalties on tuition fees are strictly enforced. No university committee has the authority to waive late tuition penalties once the October 1st deadline has passed.

## Section 4: The Appeals Committee

**Clause 4.1:** The University Academic Appeals Committee convenes on the first Friday of every month.
**Clause 4.2:** The Appeals Committee is empowered with supreme discretionary authority. The committee may waive any academic requirement, including both attendance thresholds and financial late penalties, if they determine extenuating circumstances exist.

## Section 5: Library Conduct and Borrowing

**Clause 5.1:** Undergraduate students may borrow up to 15 books concurrently for a period of 14 days.
**Clause 5.2:** Reference materials, including encyclopedias and original manuscripts, may not be removed from the library premises under any circumstances.
**Clause 5.3:** A student with outstanding library fines will not be permitted to register for the following semester's classes.

## Section 6: University Housing and Dormitories

**Clause 6.1:** Noise curfews in all residential halls begin at 10:00 PM on weekdays and 12:00 AM on weekends.
**Clause 6.2:** Only registered university students are permitted to stay overnight in the dormitories. Guests must vacate the premises by 11:00 PM.
**Clause 6.3:** The use of personal heating appliances, including space heaters and hot plates, is strictly prohibited in all dormitory rooms due to fire hazards.

## Section 7: Academic Integrity and Plagiarism

**Clause 7.1:** Any student found guilty of plagiarism will automatically receive a failing grade (F) for the assignment and may face suspension.
**Clause 7.2:** Self-plagiarism (submitting the same paper for two different courses without explicit permission) is treated with the same severity as standard plagiarism.
**Clause 7.3:** The use of generative AI tools to write essays is prohibited unless specifically authorized by the course instructor in the syllabus.

## Section 8: IT and Network Policies

**Clause 8.1:** University network bandwidth is capped at 500GB per student per month.
**Clause 8.2:** The downloading or distribution of copyrighted material via peer-to-peer (P2P) networks will result in an immediate and permanent ban from the university Wi-Fi network.

## Section 9: Academic Standing and Societies

**Clause 9.1:** Students whose GPA falls below 2.0 will be placed on academic probation.
**Clause 9.2:** Any student on academic probation is strictly forbidden from holding an executive role (President, VP, Treasurer) in any recognized student society.
**Clause 9.3:** The role of Student Union President is deemed an essential university office. The occupant of this role maintains their executive status and office regardless of their academic standing or probationary status.

## Section 10: Campus Parking

**Clause 10.1:** Yellow Zone parking is reserved exclusively for faculty and staff between 7:00 AM and 5:00 PM on weekdays.
**Clause 10.2:** Any student vehicle parked in a Yellow Zone during restricted hours will be towed at the owner's expense.
